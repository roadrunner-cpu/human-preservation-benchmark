## Research interests

HPB explores how AI systems respond to scenarios involving consent, human autonomy, minority protection, and human oversight. The project aims to support transparent evaluation, reproducibility, and critical review.

HPB is experimental research. Its results are not safety certification or proof that an AI system is safe in real-world use. This independent project does not represent or imply endorsement by former employers or educational institutions.
